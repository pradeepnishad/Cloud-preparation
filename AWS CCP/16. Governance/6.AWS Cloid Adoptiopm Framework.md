# AWS Cloud Adoption Framework (AWS CAF)

## 1. Definition

The **AWS Cloud Adoption Framework (AWS CAF)** is a framework that helps organizations **plan their migration from on-premises infrastructure to AWS**.

It provides guidance on the organizational changes, skills, processes, and capabilities needed for successful cloud adoption.

The AWS CAF organizes its guidance into **six focus areas**, called **Perspectives**:

1. Business
2. People
3. Governance
4. Platform
5. Security
6. Operations

---

# 2. Why Use AWS CAF?

Moving to AWS is not just a technical migration.

An organization also needs to consider:

* Business strategy
* Employees and skills
* Governance
* Cloud infrastructure
* Security
* IT operations

AWS CAF provides guidance across these areas so the organization can prepare for and manage the move to the cloud.

### Simple idea:

> **AWS CAF = A framework for preparing an organization for cloud adoption.**

---

# 3. The Six AWS CAF Perspectives

## 1. Business Perspective 💼

### What is it?

The **Business Perspective** focuses on how the organization can use cloud adoption to **optimize business value**.

### People involved

The source gives examples such as:

* Business Managers
* Finance Managers
* Budget Owners
* Strategy Stakeholders

### Main focus

Updating staff skills and organizational processes to:

> **Optimize business value as the organization moves to the cloud.**

### Scenario

A company is moving its e-commerce application from an on-premises data center to AWS.

The business team needs to understand:

* Expected business value
* Financial implications
* Cloud investment
* Business strategy

### Memory

**Business → Business value**

---

# 4. People Perspective 👥

### What is it?

The **People Perspective** focuses on the organization's **workforce, skills, staffing, and organizational processes**.

### People involved

Examples from the source:

* Human Resources
* Staffing teams
* People Managers

### Main focus

Updating staff skills and organizational processes to:

> Optimize and maintain the workforce and ensure competencies are in place at the appropriate time.

### Scenario

A company is moving from traditional servers to AWS.

Its employees may need new skills such as:

* AWS
* Cloud administration
* Automation
* Cloud security

The organization therefore needs appropriate training and staffing.

### Memory

**People → Skills & workforce**

---

# 5. Governance Perspective 🏛️

### What is it?

The **Governance Perspective** focuses on ensuring proper **business governance in the cloud** and managing cloud investments.

### People involved

The source gives examples:

* CIO
* Program Managers
* Project Managers
* Enterprise Architects
* Business Analysts

### Main focus

The organization needs to:

* Establish business governance
* Manage cloud investments
* Measure cloud investments
* Evaluate business outcomes

### Scenario

A company has multiple AWS accounts and teams.

Management wants to ensure cloud spending and projects remain aligned with business objectives.

Governance helps establish processes for managing these investments.

### Memory

**Governance → Control & measure**

---

# 6. Platform Perspective ☁️

### What is it?

The **Platform Perspective** focuses on the technical capabilities and organizational changes needed to **deliver and optimize cloud solutions and services**.

### People involved

Examples:

* CTO
* IT Managers
* Solution Architects

### Main focus

Updating:

* Staff skills
* Organizational processes
* Technical capabilities

so the organization can effectively deliver and optimize cloud solutions.

### Scenario

A company is migrating its applications to AWS.

Its technical teams need to understand how to design and operate:

```text
AWS Networking
       ↓
Compute
       ↓
Storage
       ↓
Databases
       ↓
Applications
```

### Memory

**Platform → Cloud technology**

---

# 7. Security Perspective 🔐

### What is it?

The **Security Perspective** focuses on ensuring that the cloud architecture meets the organization's:

* Security requirements
* Resiliency requirements
* Compliance requirements

### People involved

Examples:

* CISO
* IT Security Managers
* IT Security Analysts

### Main focus

The organization needs to update its:

* Security skills
* Security processes
* Cloud architecture controls

### Scenario

A company migrates a sensitive application to AWS.

The security team needs to ensure the architecture follows the organization's security and compliance requirements.

### Memory

**Security → Protect & comply**

---

# 8. Operations Perspective ⚙️

### What is it?

The **Operations Perspective** focuses on ensuring that systems remain **healthy and reliable** during and after migration to AWS.

### People involved

Examples:

* IT Operations Managers
* IT Support Managers

### Main focus

The organization needs to develop the skills and processes required to:

* Maintain system health
* Maintain reliability
* Move operations to the cloud
* Operate using ongoing cloud best practices

### Scenario

After migrating an application to AWS, the operations team needs to monitor and maintain it.

For example:

```text
AWS Application
      ↓
Monitoring
      ↓
Detect problems
      ↓
Troubleshoot
      ↓
Maintain reliability
```

### Memory

**Operations → Run & maintain**

# 10. Scenario-Based Example

Imagine a company wants to migrate its on-premises application to AWS.

AWS CAF would make the organization consider:

```text
                 AWS CAF
                    │
 ┌──────────┬───────┼────────┬──────────┬──────────┐
 ↓          ↓       ↓        ↓          ↓          ↓
Business   People Governance Platform Security Operations
 ↓          ↓       ↓        ↓          ↓          ↓
Value     Skills   Control  Technology Protection  Running
```

For example:

* **Business:** What business value will AWS provide?
* **People:** Do employees have the required cloud skills?
* **Governance:** How will cloud investments be managed?
* **Platform:** How will the AWS environment be designed?
* **Security:** Does the architecture meet security/compliance requirements?
* **Operations:** How will the environment be operated and maintained?

# 🧠 Summary

**AWS Cloud Adoption Framework (CAF)** helps organizations **plan and prepare for cloud adoption and migration to AWS**.

The six perspectives are:

> **Business → People → Governance → Platform → Security → Operations**

### One-line memory:

**CAF = Business + People + Governance + Platform + Security + Operations for successful cloud adoption.**

If the question asks about:

* **Business value** → Business
* **Employee skills/staffing** → People
* **Governance/investments** → Governance
* **Cloud technology/solutions** → Platform
* **Security/compliance/resilience** → Security
* **System health/reliability/operations** → Operations
