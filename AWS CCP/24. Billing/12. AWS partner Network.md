### AWS Partner Network (APN)

The **AWS Partner Network (APN)** is a **global partner program for AWS**.

Its purpose is to help organizations work with AWS and provides opportunities such as **business opportunities, exclusive training, and marketing events**.

### Two types of APN partners

**1. Consulting Partner**

* Helps companies **utilize AWS**.
* Example: helping a company migrate its infrastructure to AWS.

**2. Technology Partner**

* **Builds technology on top of AWS** as a service offering.

### APN Partner Tiers

A partner belongs to a specific tier:

* **Select**
* **Advanced**
* **Premier**

Different tiers have:

* Different **annual fee commitments**
* Different **knowledge requirements**
* Requirements involving **AWS Certifications**
* **AWS APN-exclusive certifications**

### Other benefits shown in your slide

APN partners can:

* Get **promotional AWS credits**
* Receive unique speaking opportunities through official AWS marketing channels, such as **blogs and webinars**
* Become a **sponsor with a vendor booth at AWS Events** — being part of APN is listed as a requirement for this.
