# AWS Trusted Advisor

### What is AWS Trusted Advisor?

**AWS Trusted Advisor is a recommendation tool** that automatically and actively monitors your AWS account and provides **actionable recommendations** across different categories.

A very easy way to remember it:

> **Trusted Advisor = Automated checklist of AWS best practices**

It looks at your AWS environment and tells you where you can improve.

For example, your screenshot shows a recommendation such as:

> **Security Groups – Specific Ports Unrestricted**

Meaning Trusted Advisor has detected security groups that allow unrestricted access to specific ports and recommends that you review them.

# Trusted Advisor vs Well-Architected Tool

Since you were just studying the **AWS Well-Architected Framework**, this distinction is useful.

### Well-Architected Tool

Used to **review workloads against the AWS Well-Architected Framework** and its pillars/best practices.

### Trusted Advisor

Used to **automatically monitor your AWS account and provide recommendations** across categories such as cost, performance, security, fault tolerance, and service limits.

### Simple distinction:

> **Well-Architected Tool = Review your architecture**

> **Trusted Advisor = Check your AWS environment and give recommendations**
