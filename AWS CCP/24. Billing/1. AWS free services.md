This slide is explaining an important distinction in AWS:

## 🟢 AWS Free Services vs Free Tier

The key sentence is:

> **Some AWS services are free forever, while the Free Tier is usually limited by time and/or usage.**

### 1. AWS services that are free to use

The slide lists examples such as:

* **IAM** — Identity and Access Management
* **Amazon VPC**
* **Auto Scaling**
* **AWS CloudFormation**
* **Elastic Beanstalk**
* **AWS OpsWorks**
* **AWS Amplify**
* **AWS AppSync**
* **AWS CodeStar**
* **AWS Organizations & Consolidated Billing**
* **AWS Cost Explorer**

The **service itself doesn't have a separate charge simply for existing/using it**.

---

## ⚠️ But here's the important catch

Just because a service is free **doesn't mean everything it creates is free**.

The slide specifically warns:

> These AWS services provision other services which may cost money.

For example, **CloudFormation** itself doesn't charge you for using CloudFormation.

But suppose you use CloudFormation to create:

```text
CloudFormation
      ↓
   EC2 instance
      ↓
     EBS
```

You can be charged for the **EC2 instance and EBS storage**, even though CloudFormation itself is free.

Similarly:

```text
Elastic Beanstalk
       ↓
EC2 + EBS + Load Balancer
       ↓
💰 Charges may apply
```

Elastic Beanstalk helps manage your application environment, but the underlying AWS resources can cost money.

# 🔥 Free Services vs Free Tier

This is the part I'd remember for the exam.

### Free service

The service itself doesn't charge you.

Example:

**IAM**

You don't pay simply for creating IAM users, groups, roles, or policies.

---

### Free Tier

A service is free **up to a certain usage limit**, or sometimes for a limited period.

For example, a Free Tier offering might say:

> 750 hours/month

If you exceed the applicable free amount, you can be charged.

So:

**Free ≠ unlimited**

---

## 🧠 Easy example

Imagine:

```text
IAM
 ↓
Free service
 ↓
No charge for IAM itself
```

But:

```text
CloudFormation
 ↓
Creates EC2
 ↓
EC2 runs for 100 hours
 ↓
💰 EC2 charges may apply
```

---

### ⭐ Cloud Practitioner memory trick

Think of it this way:

> **Free Service = The service itself doesn't cost money.**

> **Free Tier = A service/resource is free only within specific limits/eligibility.**

And **always check the resources a service creates**, because those underlying resources can generate charges.

This is especially relevant to your AWS account: **having a service that is "free" does not automatically mean your AWS account can never receive a bill.**
