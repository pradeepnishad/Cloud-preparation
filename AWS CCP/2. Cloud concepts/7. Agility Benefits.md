# ⚡ Agility Benefits of AWS

## 1. What is Agility?

### Definition

**Agility** is the ability to **quickly respond to changing business or technical requirements** by rapidly provisioning, modifying, testing, and deploying resources.

In AWS:

> **Agility = The ability to move, change, and experiment quickly without waiting for physical infrastructure.**

The key idea is **speed of change**.

---

# 2. Why Do We Need Agility?

In traditional IT, if you need a new server, you may have to:


Identify requirement
       ↓
Request hardware
       ↓
Get approval
       ↓
Purchase hardware
       ↓
Wait for delivery
       ↓
Install hardware
       ↓
Install OS
       ↓
Configure
       ↓
Finally start using it


This can take a significant amount of time.

With AWS:


Requirement
    ↓
Choose AWS resource
    ↓
Provision
    ↓
Configure
    ↓
Start working


Resources can generally be provisioned much faster.

# 7. Agility vs Elasticity

These two are related but **not the same**.

### ⚡ Agility

Focuses on:

> **How quickly can you respond to a change?**

Example:


Business needs new server
        ↓
Provision quickly


### 📈 Elasticity

Focuses on:

> **How quickly can resources increase/decrease as workload changes?**

Example:


Traffic ↑ → Add resources
Traffic ↓ → Remove resources


### Easy distinction:

> **Agility = Speed of change**

> **Elasticity = Resources follow demand**

---

# 8. Agility vs Scalability

### Scalability

> Ability to increase or decrease system capacity.

### Agility

> Ability to respond quickly to changing requirements.

Example:

A company suddenly launches a new product.

**Agility:**

> Quickly create the infrastructure required for the new product.

**Scalability:**

> Increase the infrastructure as the number of customers grows.
