# 🎬 Elastic Transcoder vs MediaConvert

### First: What is video transcoding?

**Transcoding = converting a video from one format/quality/codec into another format.**

Example:

```text
Original video
   ↓
1080p MP4
   ↓ Transcoding
 ┌───────────────┐
 ↓       ↓       ↓
720p    480p    Mobile
MP4     MP4     format
```

This is useful because different devices and streaming platforms may require different video formats, resolutions, codecs, and bitrates.

---

# 1. 🟠 Amazon Elastic Transcoder

The slide calls it:

> **"The Old Way"**

Elastic Transcoder was AWS's original managed video transcoding service.

### What it does

It can:

- Transcode videos
- Convert videos into **streaming formats**
- Provide APIs for programmatic transcoding

For example:

```text
Input:
movie.mov
1080p

       ↓
Elastic Transcoder

Output:
movie.mp4
720p
```

### Why does it still exist?

The slide specifically says:

> It exists due to legacy customers still using the platform.

So the important idea is:

**Elastic Transcoder = older/legacy video transcoding service.**

---

# 2. 🟠 AWS Elemental MediaConvert

The slide calls it:

> **"The New Way"**

MediaConvert is a **more robust video transcoding service**.

It can perform many different operations while transcoding.

According to your slide, it can:

### ✅ Transcode videos

Convert videos into formats suitable for streaming.

### ✅ Overlay images

You can put an image/logo/watermark over the video.

Example:

```text
        COMPANY LOGO
             ↓
┌─────────────────────────┐
│                         │
│       VIDEO             │
│                         │
└─────────────────────────┘
```

### ✅ Insert video clips

You can add another video clip into the processing workflow.

Example:

```text
Intro.mp4
   +
MainVideo.mp4
   ↓
MediaConvert
   ↓
FinalVideo.mp4
```

### ✅ Extract captions data

MediaConvert can work with caption/subtitle data as part of video processing.

### ✅ Robust UI

The slide highlights MediaConvert's more capable user interface.
