# 🛒 EC2 Reserved Instance Marketplace

## 1. Definition

The **EC2 Reserved Instance Marketplace** allows you to **sell an unused Standard Reserved Instance** if you no longer need it or cannot use it.

The basic idea is:

```text
You purchase Standard RI
        ↓
Your requirements change
        ↓
You don't need the RI anymore
        ↓
Sell it through RI Marketplace
```

This can help you recover some of the money you spent on the reservation.

---

## 2. Who Can Sell?

According to your source, the Marketplace allows you to sell **unused Standard Reserved Instances**.

Remember from the previous topic:

> **Standard RI → Can be sold on RI Marketplace**

> **Convertible RI → Cannot be sold on RI Marketplace**

---

# 3. Requirements to Sell

Your source lists several requirements.

### 1. RI must be active for at least 30 days

You can sell the RI after it has been active for:

> **At least 30 days**

And AWS must have received the applicable upfront payment.

---

### 2. US bank account

The source states that:

> You must have a **US bank account** to sell Reserved Instances on the RI Marketplace.

---

### 3. At least one month remaining

There must be:

> **At least one month remaining** on the RI term.

---

# 4. What Happens Before It's Sold?

The source says you continue to receive the **pricing and capacity benefits** of the reservation until the sale is completed.

So:

```text
RI listed for sale
       ↓
Still yours
       ↓
Continue receiving applicable benefits
       ↓
Sale completed
       ↓
Transferred to buyer
```

---

# 5. What Can the Seller Change?

According to the source, the seller can set the **upfront selling price**.

However, the following remain the same as when the RI was originally purchased:

* Usage price
* Instance type
* Availability Zone
* Platform
* Other configuration

So:

```text id="t4v6xg"
Seller
  ↓
Can set upfront price
```

But:

```text id="t4v6xg"
Instance type
AZ
Platform
Usage price
  ↓
Remain unchanged
```

---

# 6. Remaining Term

The Marketplace displays the remaining term rounded **down to the nearest month**.

Example from your source:

```text
9 months + 15 days remaining
        ↓
Marketplace displays
        ↓
9 months
```

---

# 7. Selling Limit

Your source states:

> You can sell up to **$20,000 of Reserved Instances per year**.

If you need to sell more than that, you need to go through the applicable AWS process for increasing the limit.

---

# 8. GovCloud

The source specifically states:

> Reserved Instances in the **GovCloud Region cannot be sold** on the Reserved Instance Marketplace.

For your exam notes:

```text
GovCloud RI
    ↓
Cannot be sold
on RI Marketplace
```
