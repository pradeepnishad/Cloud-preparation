### AWS Free Tier

AWS Free Tier allows you to use certain AWS services **at no cost** within specific limits.

There are two types shown:

* **Free for the first 12 months after signup**
* **Free usage up to a monthly limit forever**

### 🧠 Exam shortcut

Remember:

**12 months → certain services have introductory free usage**

**Forever → some services have a recurring free monthly allowance**

And **Free Tier does NOT mean every AWS service is completely free**. You need to stay within the specified usage limits.
